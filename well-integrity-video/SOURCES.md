# Sources and fact base

Facts in the narration were checked against the sources below (research done October 2026).
Well geometry, pressures and plotted values in the animations are **illustrative** and not for engineering use.

## Regulations and standards (Norway)
- Havtil (Norwegian Ocean Industry Authority, called Petroleumstilsynet / PSA until 1 Jan 2024):
  - Facilities Regulations § 48 Well barriers: https://www.havtil.no/en/regulations/all-acts/the-facilities-regulations3/VIII/48/
  - Activities Regulations § 85 Well barriers: https://www.havtil.no/en/regulations/all-acts/the-activities-regulations3/XV/85/
  - Activities Regulations § 88 Securing wells (12-month monitoring rule for subsea wells), Management Regulations § 5 Barriers
- Standards Norway, NORSOK D-010 *Well integrity in drilling and well operations*, edition 5 (2021), consolidated
  NORSOK D-010:2021+AC:2024: https://online.standard.no/en/norsok-d-010-2021ac2-2021ac-2024
  - Rev. 5 changes (barrier requirements matrix, creeping formation and non-cement materials, PWC, MPD chapter,
    BOP test matrix with 14-day interval kept, technology qualification): ABL training summary
    https://abl-group.com/abl/abl-training/norsok-d-010-well-integrity-standard/introduction-to-the-norsok-d-010-well-integrity-standard/ ,
    Hydrawell https://blog.hydrawell.no/norsok-d-10-embrace-hydrawell-pwc-technology
  - Test criteria (low pressure 15–20 bar ≥ 5 min, static test ≥ 10 min stable, zero leak unless EAC states otherwise,
    70 bar differential in production phase, inflow test ≥ 30 min) and plug lengths (open hole 100 m with ≥ 50 m above
    inflow source; cased hole 50 m on foundation, otherwise 100 m), as reported in published extracts of D-010.
- Offshore Norge Guideline 117, Recommended guidelines for well integrity (rev. 6, 2017):
  https://www.offshorenorge.no/contentassets/e8f7a98e933b43feb76f823097e2e7b8/117---offshore-norge---recommended-guidelines-well-integrity---rev-6.pdf
- Offshore Norge Guideline 135, Classification and categorisation of well control and well integrity incidents (rev. 07):
  https://www.offshorenorge.no/contentassets/ee44492c386941f88486093bdd7778e3/135---recommended-guidelines-for-classification-and-categorisation-rev-07-final.pdf
- ISO 16530-1:2017 Well integrity – Part 1: Life cycle governance.
- API RP 14B (SCSSV leak criterion 15 scf/min gas or 400 cc/min liquid).
